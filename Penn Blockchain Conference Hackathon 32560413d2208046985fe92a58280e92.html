Getting to Penn Museum

The Penn Museum is located at 3260 South Street, Philadelphia, PA 19104, on Penn’s campus. It’s easily accessible by walking, rideshare, or public transit. If you’re coming by SEPTA or AMTRAK/regional rail, the Museum is a short walk from 30th Street Station and nearby trolley stops. Limited street parking and garage parking may be available nearby, so public transit or rideshare is recommended when possible.
